Book Marker

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your sign-in details and the books, quotes and words you save.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • Deleting your account in the app permanently deletes all of your data from our servers and your device.

1. Who we are

Book Marker is an iOS app for keeping track of the books you read, the quotes you love and the words you learn. In this policy “we” and “us” mean the developer of Book Marker, and “you” means anyone using the app.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we store your email address and a unique account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an identifier and either your email address or a private relay address, if you choose Hide My Email. Email passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider. We never see or store them in plain text.

Content you add

The books on your shelves (title, author, cover and shelf), your quotes, your vocabulary words and definitions, and any page numbers or notes you attach, along with the dates they were added and changed. This is synced to your account so it's available when you sign in.

Photos for quote capture

If you choose to capture a quote with the camera, the photo of the page you highlighted is sent to our server and passed to Google's Gemini service so it can read the highlighted text back to you. We do not store the photo. It is used for that single request and then discarded. Only the text you choose to save becomes part of your account. The camera is only used when you open it yourself.

Usage records for quote capture

Each time you use quote capture we record the time and your account ID. We use this only to enforce fair-use limits, and it is deleted with your account.

Searches and lookups

When you search for a book or look up a word, your search text is sent to the book and dictionary services listed in section 5. We don't save your search history to your account.

Technical information

Like any internet service, our hosting provider automatically receives technical details with each request, such as your IP address, the time and the type of request. These are used for security and reliability and are kept only in short-lived service logs.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photo library, health or financial information.

4. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To store and sync your books, quotes and words across your devices.
  • To turn a highlighted photo into quote text when you ask us to.
  • To find books and definitions when you search.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service running.

We only use your information to provide the app's features. We never use it for marketing or profiling.

6. How we protect your information

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Our database enforces row-level security, so each account can only ever access its own data. Keys for third-party services stay on our servers and are never shipped in the app. On your device, your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and fix issues quickly.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. You're always in control of it:

  • Delete an item: deleting a book, quote or word in the app also deletes it from our servers the next time the app syncs.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, including your email address, sign-in identities, books, quotes, vocabulary words, notes and quote-capture usage records, from our servers, and erases the app's data from your device. It cannot be undone.
  • Sign out: signing out removes your data from that device. It stays in your account for next time.

After deletion, copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a short time until those backups expire on their normal schedule. They aren't used for anything and aren't restored except to recover from a disaster.

If you can't access the app, contact us (section 11) and we'll delete your account for you.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in the EU and UK and the CCPA in California, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we process it. You can view, edit and delete your content directly in the app. For anything else, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. We will never treat you differently for exercising these rights.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your information to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract) and, for security and abuse prevention, for our legitimate interests.

9. Children

Book Marker is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. International users

Our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, those transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
